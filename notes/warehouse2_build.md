# Warehouse 2: build log (2026-10-27, main PC map builder)

Work order (Lead's plan, approved by Edward): a new 1v1 match map "Warehouse 2", a grounded GTA-style freight warehouse on the
old Warehouse's cover layout, roll-up garage openings at both ends onto closed loading yards. The old Warehouse is untouched and
stays the Tutorial map. Brief: `briefs/map-warehouse2.md`. Kit list for the modeler: `briefs/kit-warehouse2.json`.
Sheet: `notes/warehouse2_sheet.jpg` (old left / new right of each pair, cameras `notes/warehouse_study/cams.json`).

Built and tested in `out/warehouse2/Warehouse2_build.rbxl` (a copy of `Matchmaking_place.rbxl` taken 02:38, no newer lock).
No repo-root place, port_prep or live place was opened or saved. The build copy was never saved either: the installer is the
deliverable and rebuilds everything.

## How it is built
- `fix/map_warehouse2.lua` (match places only; refuses to run in the lobby): builds `Workspace.Warehouse2` at plan origin
  (-84.2, 0.4, -300) and the registry folder `ReplicatedStorage.DuelMaps.Warehouse2` (DisplayName "Warehouse 2", Modes 1v1,
  MapInfo with its own Lighting), plus MaterialVariants `WH2_*` from our own uploads (tex/asset_ids.json). Idempotent; a
  `Warehouse2` it didn't build goes to `ServerStorage._Warehouse2_Backup`; `UNDO = true` removes Warehouse2, the entry and the
  variants. `Workspace.Warehouse` is only read (its `Shootables` are cloned).
- The layout block is generated by `tools/wh2_gen.py` from `notes/warehouse_study/warehouse_dump.json` (never hand-edit it):
  32 cover pieces, both spawn markers at full precision, the old camera skin (30 of 36 rects; the 6 inside the wider doors
  dropped), the shootables' positions for read-back.
- Gameplay layer (all invisible): `CollisionProxies` = `COLP_<id>` for every cover piece (old names, positions, sizes, cover
  role; material = new surface for the bullet sound: crates WoodPlanks, pallet stacks Cardboard, block walls / barriers
  Concrete), `COLP_Floor`, `COLP_Wall_*` (side walls, end walls either side of the 20-wide doors, headers, wedge gables),
  `COLP_Ceiling_E/W` (pitched, on the roof sheets' underside), `COLP_Yard_*` (floors, trailers, container stacks, fences).
  Every non-cover proxy is NotCover. `CamSkin` = the old skin's rectangles and names. `Bounds` = OutOfBounds caps 12/17..60
  over the yard edges (collide, no query). `Shootables` = the Warehouse's clone (FireBarrel_F1-F4, SteamValve_S1-S2 with tags
  and attributes). `MatchSpawns.SpawnA/B`. `Props` = PCOL_* blockers (collide, no query) under the clutter piles, as the old map.
- Map attributes: `Builder`, `LayoutVersion 1`, `SteamFill = true` (the systems agent's steam-fill contract), `PlanOrigin`.
- Look (Studio Parts + own textures, parts-first): concrete slab with joints, cream bay / aisle / walkway lines, hatch + KEEP
  CLEAR at each door, stencilled bay numbers, soft oil stains (overlapping GUI discs, no image upload), tyre scuffs (grime
  texture); blockwork walls with a dark kick band and one olive stripe, piers on the old skin rectangles, steel columns in the
  slots, galvanised corrugated cladding above with a glazed band; steel portal frames (6 frames, knee braces, end rafters,
  gable posts), purlins, corrugated roof with two skylight strips a side, ridge cap, gutters; roll-up doors 20 x 14 (steel
  reveal, drum housing, guides, bottom bar, olive "LOADING BAY 01/02" sign, threshold plate, wall-pack floods outside);
  19 high-bay lamps (bell, driver, rod, dim warm Neon lens 70,64,54, a SpotLight pool + a weak PointLight fill each; the two
  door-row centre lamps were dropped so the LOADING BAY signs show), a warm door light under each roll-up hood onto the hatch, a
  flood on each end wall's outside onto the trailer framed by the door; wall dressing (4 electrical cabinets on the old 1.6
  bumps, steam main from each valve's riser along the wall, signs, extinguishers); yards: weathered apron, drain gully, stains,
  bollards, a 40-ft dry-van trailer along the back, two stacked 20-ft containers along one side, a screened palisade fence with
  barbed wire, neighbour sheds and poles beyond. South yard = north yard turned 180 degrees, then recoloured so the ends differ:
  south (A's end, BAY 01) drab-olive (108,118,84) "OLIVE LINE HAULAGE" trailer with a cream stripe, grey under sand containers; north (B's end,
  BAY 02) cream "TACTICAL FREIGHT CO." trailer with an olive stripe, rust under green containers. Big stencilled 01 / 02 on the
  blockwork either side of each door (readable from mid-hall).
- Lighting (MapInfo.Lighting, applied by RoundManager when the map wins the vote; = the brief): ClockTime 14.3, latitude 35,

## Lessons for the vault (no game names; for the keeper / gardener)
1. **Rebuilding a map's look on the same gameplay layer: carry the camera skin too.** A study's free floor counts cells near the
   walls; a new wall that stands proud everywhere (where the old skin had notches) shifts "floor seen from spawn" by 6-10 %
   though the sight lines are identical. Build the new walls on the old skin's rectangles.
2. **Never round gameplay markers when transcribing them.** Spawn markers 0.00007 off a half-stud boundary flipped the analysis'
   spawn cell: keep source values at full precision in generated layouts.
3. **Floor overlays float, they don't sit:** MapAudit ZFIGHT compares same-facing planes within 0.02, so every flat thing
   resting at floor level (paint lines, hatch carriers, pallet bottoms, skids, barrier slots) shares the floor plane with the
   others. Give overlays a 0.03 lift and keep 0.02+ between stacked overlay planes.
4. **A concrete texture set can render near-black in shade** (a low-roughness map makes it reflect the dark interior):
   measure the render (here 50 vs 119 with the plain material) before using a set on large shaded faces.
5. **Down-facing SpotLights leave every vertical face that looks away from a lamp black** under ShadowMap (no bounce): one soft
   PointLight per high-bay lamp (0.45 / 26) lit the cover sides and kept the floor grey.
6. **Floor text orientation on a Top-face SurfaceGui:** at carrier yaw 0 the text reads along world -Z (letter tops -X); yaw
   90 = -X, 180 = +Z, -90 = +X. Turn the invisible carrier, not the label.
7. **Point-symmetric yards:** build one, clone and turn it 180 degrees about the hall centre (rename _N_ -> _S_, recolour) so
   both teams' back yards are identical in play and different to look at.
8. **The match place's test "bot" is a dummy:** a me + bot Play test proves spawns, rounds and kills (via the test hook), not
   AI behaviour; say so in the report.
9. **A MapAudit wrapper must pass `indoor` (or set the map's `Indoor` attribute) or SKY silently never runs;** on a map with
   open-air parts and query-less art, prove "no hole to the sky" with your own upward sweep (art made queryable in Play only).
10. **Prove enclosure with a flood fill, not drop tags:** tagging boundary floors KitIntendedDrop silences HOLE exactly where the
    proof is needed.
11. **A point-symmetric clone needs a different landmark at each end:** recolour every part of the clone (posts, rails, roof),
    then compare the same camera at both ends on the render.
16. **Door signs above a roller hood / drum: check them from door distance,** not only from the centre: keep the sign's bottom
    edge above the hood's top as projected from the nearest player eye.
14. **Measure the landmark against every emissive in the same frame from the area's centre:** a "dim" Neon swatch (118,110,94)
    still rendered 150-170 under bloom; a landmark framed around a dark object (a dark trailer in a door) reads as a hole.
15. **Walk-through scan:** sample the centre of every visible art part at body height (1.5 / 2.5 / 3.2) inside the walkable area
    against the colliding parts; small props (bollards, cartons, extinguishers, chairs, column flanges) are the usual misses.
12. **SurfaceGui text:** setting `TextWrapped = false` turns `TextScaled` off; `TextScaled` stops at 100 px, so big lettering
    needs a low `PixelsPerStud` (100 px / pps = the largest letter in studs).
13. **Even interior light is the default failure under ShadowMap:** a PointLight per lamp lights everything the same; a downward
    SpotLight pool + a weak PointLight fill gives pools (measure under / between >= 1.3) without black cover sides.

- Lesson (grader 6b): on a point-symmetric map, never even out one face with different light values at the two ends; keep the
  values equal and compare mirrored cameras (p50 within ~10 %). A local face that still differs is a renderer voxel artefact.
