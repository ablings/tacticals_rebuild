## Rules (06-08)
- 10 placement duels unlock your rank; then you gain/lose ELO; promote/demote across rank lines.
- ELO Shield: 1 free per day once ranked, protects one duel's loss; none at 1,800+.
- 3,000+ must play once every 7 days; ELO decay = -100 per inactive day.
- Gameplay: strict competitive map pool; 3 maps shown in voting; each team bans 1 map and 2 weapons (defaults bannable only if every
  player owns >4 weapons in that slot); third person off; aim assist -50%; auto-shoot restricted; max 2 weapon switches.
- Rewards at season end: Glory (from wins + final ELO, spent in a Ranked Shop), a rank charm engraved with the season number,
  Archnemesis gets a limited Mythical skin; during the season the Contract gives cosmetics/Glory/a skin for winning.
