# RIVALS grenade cook timing (measured 2026-10-21, main PC, Edward playing the RIVALS range)

Method: the Roblox window recorded at about 10 fps (each frame named by its ms timestamp, about 0.11 s between frames). Edward held
the throw until the grenade blew up in his hand, twice. Sheets: rivals_grenade_cook1.jpg (6.0-12.7 s), rivals_grenade_cook2.jpg (20-27 s).

| | first frame of the cocked windup pose | in-hand explosion | cook time |
|---|---|---|---|
| cook 1 | 9.45 s (grenade still rising at 9.35) | 10.52 s | 1.07 s (1.17 s from the rise) |
| cook 2 | 21.28 s (rising at 21.17) | 22.27 s | 0.99 s (1.10 s from the rise) |

**In-hand fuse = about 1.1 s (+/-0.1 s) from pressing to the blast.** The key press itself isn't visible, so the true start can be
up to one frame (0.1 s) before the first windup frame.
- About 0.1 s before it goes off, a translucent white SPHERE/ring grows around the grenade (a blast-radius warning; 10.30 s, 22.16 s).
- The blast: a white-hot star burst (1 frame), then an orange fireball + black smoke (about 0.3 s), then a red damage tint on the screen edges.
- Both times a second, big WHITE wash-out came about 1.4 s after the blast (11.91 s, 23.66 s), with a light beam. Not sure what
  it is (respawn / heal flash / a second grenade?). Treat it as unexplained.
- The fuse of a THROWN grenade wasn't measurable: thrown ones exploded off-screen with no flash in frame.
For the exact constant, read the grenade fuse from RIVALS' scripts in the reference place (read-only), like the UI did for the scope.
