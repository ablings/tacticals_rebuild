## 1. RIVALS' shooting range: contents and dimensions (measured)

**Where and how you enter.** It is a separate floating box in the sky (`Workspace.Lobby.ShootingRange`, centre about
1380, -716, 1044). It is not part of the lobby. `Lobby.Hub.Extra.Range` is only hub scenery (a hill, palms, an annex; see
`rivals_hub_range_annex.png`), not the range itself. You enter from a lobby button or by "try weapon" in Equipment. You
arrive on one of **2 spawn pads in the middle of the arena**, set either side of two **LEAVE** walls. You leave through a
LEAVE wall or the "Leave the Shooting Range" button. A lobby screen lists who is in the range.

**Footprint** (`rivals_top.png`, `rivals_34.png`)
| Piece | Size (studs) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Arena | **250 x 245** floor (one 250x5x245 Part), perimeter walls **50** tall | open sky; invisible walls 320 tall plus an invisible lid ~235 above the floor |
| Distance gallery | **180 x 70**, walls 55 tall | joins the middle of the arena's east wall; floor in **7 coloured 25-stud bands** |
| Walkable surface | **~59,000** in the arena: floor ~30,450 visible + block roofs at **+50** ~21,300 + tower tops ~7,000; gallery ~9,600 | so about **half of all walkable area is on the roofs** |

**Contents** (`ShootingRangeEntities` + `ShootingRange.Extra/Important`)
| Item | Count | Placement / behaviour (as a player sees it) |
|---|---|---|
| Dummy (R15, **150 HP**) | **25** | 7 in the gallery (one at every 25 studs, 25..175); 18 in the arena: **9 on the floor and 9 on block roofs (+50)**. Stands still, pinned in place. |
| DPS dummy (**infinite HP**, DPS readout overhead) | **10** | 6 in the gallery's second lane (25..175, no 150); 4 on the arena floor. Pinned so knockback cannot move it. |
| Floating target (**1 HP**, bullseye, always faces you) | **172** | scattered over the whole arena at 8..128 above the floor; most targets by count |
| Distance signs | **13** | invisible boards with "25..175 / studs away" text over each gallery dummy |
| Blocks/walls by height | 7 towers 75-100 tall, **58 blocks 40-55 tall** (roofs walkable), 78 mid 10-35, 42 low 4-9, 88 thin slabs (bridges, ledges, trim), 22 trusses | about 309 visible Parts + 11 invisible collision Parts; **1,179 grid Textures** (5x5, all faces) |

**Density.** 35 humanoid targets + 172 floating targets on ~74k studs² of footprint: **0.47 humanoids per 1,000 studs²**
overall. That is 0.36 in the arena and 1.0 in the gallery. Add 2.8 floating targets per 1,000 studs² of arena. The arena feels
full because of height (50-stud blocks, towers, ropes, targets in the air), not because the floor is crowded with dummies.
Measured: **no moving targets and no pop-up targets** anywhere in the range.

## 2. Tacticals' Training today (from the scripts + survey)

- **Room:** x -88..88, z -121..121 (**172 x 238 = 40,936 studs²**), floor y 600, ceiling 636.5 (+36), one level, enclosed
  (walls with glass strips). There are 4 quadrants split by Div_X/Div_Z walls with windows, and a centre HubRing/HubBeam.
  Arena: 209 Parts. Cover per quadrant: 10 blocks, 12 low walls, 6 pillars and 4 walls, so **128 cover pieces**. There are
  12 lights and the TRAINING sign meshes at (0..2, 626, 0).
- **Entry:** heli cutscene (ExfilFX) or PLAY > TRAINING. The `Events.Training.Enter` event makes the server set `InTraining`,
  call `LoadCharacter` and pivot you to a **random `Training.Arrivals` child** (16 now; fallback `TrainingArrive`). You get
  your loadout weapon on spawn. Leaving: LEAVE button or [M], which takes you to the hangar (112, 3.5, 0). Dying in training
  sends you back to an arrival point.
- **Dummies (`TrainingManager`):** `ENEMIES = 24` alive. They are clones of `ServerStorage.RangeAssets.Dummy`, painted red
  with no face, **150 HP**, and anchored (static). Each one goes on a **random free `Training.EnemySpots` child** (336 spots
  now, so 24 of 336 are filled at any time). Placement: the dummy is centred on the spot's X/Z, its feet are at spot.Y - 3, and
  it takes the spot's rotation. A hit shows a damage number, colour-coded by zone. The dummy heals 2.5 s after the last hit.
  When killed it ragdolls ("ELIMINATED"), fades after 3 s, and respawns 2 s later on another random free spot.
- **Safety logic:** in training, falling below **y 585** (FLOOR_Y - 15) sends you to an arrival point. A non-training player
  above **y 540** is sent to the hangar and has their tools removed. The lobby fall-catch below y -25 does not apply to
  training players.
- **Cover:** every CanCollide part without the `NotCover` attribute is cover (CoverController). Shots check line of sight by
  raycast against everything except characters (WeaponUtil.IsPathClear). Roblox's own jump is off (JumpHeight 0): **Space
  hops 2.2 studs or vaults low cover**, so players **cannot jump onto anything taller than a low wall**.
- **Lights:** parts tagged `RoomLight` with Room = Training switch on only while the camera is **within 175 of (0, 606, 0)**.
- **Weapon ranges:** 200-600 studs. Damage falloff ends at 50-250, so distance markers out to **200** are useful.

| | RIVALS range | Tacticals now |
|---|---|---|
| Footprint | 250x245 + 180x70 (~73,850) | 172x238 (40,936) |
| Levels | floor + roofs at +50 + towers | one level, ceiling +36 |
| Humanoid targets alive | 35 (25 + 10 DPS), fixed places | 24, random among 336 spots |
| Floating targets | 172 | 0 |
| Cover/blocks | ~280 visible pieces, mostly tall | 128 cover pieces, mostly low/mid |
| Distance markers | gallery 25..175 | none |
| Lights | 0 | 12 |

## 3. Proposed Training v2 (all within the existing scripts)

**Footprint** (`plan_new.png`): an **arena of 256 x 256** (x -128..128, z -128..128) plus a **distance gallery of 224 x 72**
off the middle of the east wall (x 128..352, z -36..36). That is **81,664 studs² of footprint: 2.0x today and about 1.1x
RIVALS**, plus a **roof deck at y 624 (+24)**. Floor top stays at **y 600**, still in the same place, centred on the origin.
Nothing goes below y 585 or is reachable by lobby players above y 540.
**Open sky like RIVALS:** perimeter walls 36 tall (today's height) with grid, then invisible walls up to ~y 760 and an invisible
lid. The current ceiling is dropped (question Q2).

**Zones**
| Zone | Where | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| Spawn plaza | centre 40x40, olive-edged pad | **16 Arrive parts on a ring (r 14) facing outward** (they replace the 16 at the room edges) plus `TrainingArrive` at the centre facing east; the TRAINING sign above it as a landmark; an "[M] LEAVE" floor stencil |
| Boulevards | 40-wide cross from the plaza to each wall | low cover only, so sight lines stay open; the east boulevard runs straight into the gallery |
| Distance gallery | east, 224 x 72, 2 lanes 32 wide | firing line at x 136 (2 low counters + olive Neon line); **an EnemySpot at every 25 studs, 25..200, in both lanes (16)**; a distance sign over each one (16); alternating sage / warm-grey 25-stud floor bands instead of RIVALS' rainbow |
| NW block maze + roof deck | x -128..-20, z 20..128 | blocks 24 tall with walkable roofs, 12-20-wide streets, roof bridges (slabs), stair ramp + truss ladders up; dummies on the floor and on the roofs |
| NE towers + overwatch | x 20..128, z 20..128 | 3 landmark towers 60 tall, an overwatch block (+24, ramp) facing the plaza and the gallery mouth, tall walls |
| SW cover / peek course | x -128..-20, z -128..-20 | Tacticals' own cover vocabulary in rows (low walls, blocks, pillars, tall walls), so you practise cover-to-cover and peeking at dummies 20-60 away |
| SE open free-fire field | x 20..128, z -128..-20 | open floor with **olive floor lines at 25/50/75/100** from the plaza edge + signs; scattered low cover; dummies at each distance |

With 24 alive on 44 spots, about 55% of the spots are filled at any time; with 35 alive, 80%. Because the spots are few and
hand-placed, the room reads like RIVALS' fixed dummy layout without a code change.

**Construction (Parts first, grid-tile style).** Plain Parts, as RIVALS does it and as today's Training is built. Every face
carries our own **TX_overlay_grid** (`tex/hub_overlay_grid.png`, already own work): 5x5 studs, black, 0.75 transparency, all
six faces. Sizes snap to 1 stud (2 studs for blocks) so the grid lines up from part to part. Each type of piece keeps its own
name (`Block`, `LowWall`, `Pillar`, `Wall`, `Tower`, `Bastion`, `Bridge`, `Ramp`) inside `Training.Arena`.
**Palette** (Tacticals accents in place of RIVALS purple/gold; base tones per `rivals_style.md` §5):
- Floor: warm grey 200,196,186. Blocks/walls: sage 167,173,152 and RIVALS' neutral 151,153,163 (or a warm-shifted version).
  Bastions/towers: dark olive 107,122,58 trim bands. Perimeter top cap: near-black 10,12,10.
- Lines only, never fills: olive 138,156,74 Neon for the firing line, distance lines and plaza edge. Cream 236,232,218 for
  sign text. The dummies stay red (196,40,40, set by the script).
- **Match the exact tile colours of the current Training** (Edward's visual standard). Sample them in Step 2. The survey
  captures are too dark to read them from.

## 4. Hooks: what the scripts read (add more of these, no code change)
| Script | Reads | Rule for the new room |
|---|---|---|
| TrainingManager | `Workspace.LobbyMap.Training` (Folder) | keep the name and path; dummies are parented here |
| TrainingManager | `Training.Arrivals` (any number of BaseParts) | **the part's CFrame is used as the character pivot**: centre ~3.5 above the surface, facing = the part's LookVector; Anchored, CanCollide/CanTouch/CanQuery off, Transparency 1 |
| TrainingManager | `Training.TrainingArrive` | fallback; keep one |
| TrainingManager | `Training.EnemySpots` (any number of BaseParts) | **spot centre = standing surface + 3** (works on roofs too); **rotate each spot to face the shooting position** (the dummy copies its rotation); 1-stud invisible, non-colliding, CanQuery off |
| TrainingManager | `ServerStorage.RangeAssets.Dummy` | unchanged |
| TrainingManager / FallCatch | y 585 / y 540 | lowest walkable surface ≥ 600, all content above 540 belongs to training: satisfied |
| CoverController | CanCollide + no `NotCover` = cover | put **`NotCover` on** invisible walls/lid, trusses, ramps, bridges, sign boards, floor bands/lines |
| CombatManager / WeaponUtil.IsPathClear | raycast vs everything except characters | **sign boards: CanCollide off, CanQuery off** so they never block shots; keep gallery lanes clear |
| RoomFX | `RoomLight` tag, Room = Training, camera within 175 of (0,606,0) | RIVALS uses 0 lights; with open sky we plan **no RoomLights** (Q4). Any we do add must sit near the centre: at the corners (181) and in the gallery (x > 175) they switch off |
| ExfilFX / remotes | `Events.Training.Enter/Leave`, `InTraining` | unchanged |

## 5. What stays / what goes
Stays: entry and exit (heli + PLAY card, LEAVE/[M]), dummy behaviour (150 HP, damage numbers, heal, ragdoll, respawn), the
cover system, weapons, safety heights, the TRAINING sign (moved over the plaza), the grid-tile look.
Goes: the 336-spot grid (becomes 44 hand-placed spots), the 4-quadrant divider walls and HubRing (replaced by the plaza +
boulevards), the ceiling (open sky), the 12 lights (Q4).

## 6. Open questions for Edward
1. **Dummies alive: 24 or 35?** 35 matches RIVALS. It needs a one-line change in TrainingManager (`ENEMIES = 24` → 35). Without
   it, 24 alive on 44 spots.
2. **Open sky (RIVALS) or keep a ceiling?** With a roof deck at +24, a ceiling at +36 leaves only 12 studs. The proposal is
   open sky with invisible walls.
3. **Fixed gallery dummies?** Today's spawning is random, so the gallery holds ~9 of its 16 on average (55%). Always filling
   the gallery needs a small script change (e.g. spots tagged `Fixed` always refill first). Accept random?
4. **Lights:** none, like RIVALS (and the RoomFX 175 radius), or a few near the plaza?
5. **Code-only RIVALS content.** Each of these needs script work; leave it out for now?
   - the **172 floating 1-HP targets** (RIVALS' biggest target count)
   - **DPS dummies** (infinite HP + DPS readout)
   - **jump pads**, which we replace with ramps/trusses since Tacticals has no real jump
   - **tightropes**
   - **"change weapon" stations**. The Kit prompt is a lobby UI, which is hidden in training, so this is unverified and probably
     needs code.
6. **Height of the roof level:** +24 (proposal: reachable by ramp/truss in Tacticals' movement) or RIVALS' +50?
7. **Size:** 256 x 256 + a 224 gallery (2x today), or larger to match RIVALS' walkable total once the roofs count?
