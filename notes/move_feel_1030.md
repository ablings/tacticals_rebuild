## Part A: movement feel (fix/move_feel_1030.lua)
Generated by `tools/move_feel_build.py` from `fix/move_feel_1030/*` (round 1; two-pass, round-stamped on MovementConfig
`MoveFeel1030Round`, every anchor checked first, UNDO exact: back to the pristine hash fbf778ad, then reinstall).

| Piece | What |
|---|---|
| MoveFeelConfig (RS.Modules.Shared.Config) | every knob below; `Enabled = false` turns the pass off |
| MoveFeelController (Client.Controllers) | momentum (own body), lean (every body), foot planting IK (every body near you), camera post-process (own), Studio test hook `MoveFeelTestHook` |
| MovementController edits | crouch / slide fades from the config; crouch loops over the gun Hold (LocoPriority); buffered slide (in the air / last 0.2 s of the cooldown); the slide keeps its speed when it turns the body (was a one-frame stall 25 -> 7 studs/s) |
| CoverController edits | Space in the air replays on touchdown (0.18 s); Space mid-vault = climb / pull-up on landing (0.35 s); move-clip fade-in 0.08 -> 0.12 (knob) |

Behaviour:
- **Momentum:** the move input eases in (AccelTime 0.11 s) and out (DecelTime 0.08 s: +1.0 stud of stop at a 25 studs/s
  sprint); its direction swings at 900 deg/s at walk, 420 at sprint (turn rate tied to speed); a reversal over 120 deg brakes
  through standing. Skipped whenever something else drives you (cover, cover moves, slide, cutscene, controls off). Gameplay
  speeds unchanged (test-checked: Walk 10, Sprint 20, Crouch 4.8, Slide 24/20/0.75/0.6, PullUp 0.5, Peek 0.115, Hop 2.2).
- **Lean:** waist pitch 0.045 deg per stud/s^2 forward (max 5 fwd / 3 back), roll 0.06 deg per stud/s^2 sideways (max 5);
  off while the gun is raised (muzzle on line), in cover, mid-move, in the air. Every client draws it for every body.
- **Feet:** standing / slow (< 3 studs/s) on stairs and slopes the hips sink to the lower foot's ground (max 0.9) and each
  foot reaches its own ground (IKControl, knee pole forward); flat floor costs 2 raycasts per body and no IK.
- **Camera (own, undone each frame):** stride bob 0.035 walk / 0.075 sprint (aim keeps 25 %, scoped none), sprint roll 0.35;
  landing dip up to 0.35 studs + 1.2 deg nod from 14 studs/s; FOV +4 sprint / +5 slide / +4 dive; slide drop 0.35 + roll 2;
  dive roll 3. Reduced motion = the existing **Settings "Camera Shake" off** (no new setting name): all of it off.
- **Which clips own the legs (found + fixed):** every gun clip keys the legs in a fixed pose (Hold / Lowered at Action;
  Fire / Reload / Draw / Inspect / Holster at Action4), so with a gun out the walk / strafe / sprint clips (Movement) could
  not move the legs: **the body glided with frozen legs** (hip angle constant 9.18 deg while walking). The loops now play
  at Action2 (`LocoPriority`), over Hold / Lowered.
- **Locomotion speed sync:** AnimFinalConfig.Loco.Authored set from measured foot drift (`LocoAuthored`).

`art/anim/reload_variants/install_sources.lua`.
