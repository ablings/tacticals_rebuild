# Movement / cover / shooting rework [MOVE1029] (2026-10-29, main PC, systems session)

Edward's five changes (plan by the Lead, approved by Edward; his go to publish to every Tacticals place).

## What changed
| # | Change | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wall cover per map** | `MoveRules.IsWallCover`: a NotCover part with `WallProxy = true` is tall cover when the nearest ancestor with a `WallCover` attribute says true (MapInfo `WallCover = true` + the map model's attribute; lobby pad duels: the Solid layer's attribute). House on the **Test place only**: `fix/house_wallcover.lua` marks its 67 room-wall proxies (Role "wall", not the `_Above` lintels or `_Win_` sills); windows, barriers, door leaves and NotCover obstacles stay no cover. The code ships everywhere; no live map turns it on. |
| 2 | **One vault** | Space at vaultable low cover always does the fast vault (`VaultTime`), sprinting or not. Gone: `SilentVaultSlowdown`, `SilentVaultCoverReach`, the 16-stud sneak into cover after a vault, Tutorial 1's "Space alone is off". Kept: Q mid-vault lands in cover (`VaultCoverReach`), the revault. The AI hears a vault (`VaultNoiseRange` 24, lost in steam like a reload): before, it heard no vault at all (the body's speed reads 0 mid-vault). Tutorial 2's line and objective rewritten. |
| 3 | **Pull-up climb** | Space, standing or mid-hop, facing any surface whose top is <= the standing head top over your floor (`PullUpMaxHeight` 5.1, measured 5.09) with a 1.4 x 1.4 footing and headroom: grab and pull up. 4.0-5.1 high: the animator's PullUp clip + its root path; lower: the Climb clip. MovementManager re-checks every one with its own rays (ledge there, not NoClimb, footing, headroom, no ceiling over the way up, no window barrier); a refusal is flagged (`MoveFlags`, logged) and the body goes back. `NoClimb = true` on a part or an ancestor = never climbed (`fix/noclimb_1029.lua`). |
| 4 | **Lean** | At the full pose of a side peek (tall cover edge, low cover side-peek end), the key toward the edge you can't slide past (A at a left edge, D at a right edge; the stick toward it; a Lean button on phones shown only then) leans you out. Server: character attribute `Lean` (-1/0/1); every client draws it (the animator's LeanLeft/Right(+Crouch) clips, a procedural waist roll where no clip plays); HitRewind rewinds it (`MoveRules.LeanPart`, counted once if the server's own body already shows it). Camera rolls 4 deg and slides 0.6. |
| 5 | **No shooting before the peek turn finishes** | Peek pose 0.18 -> 0.115 s, in-cover turn 1000 -> 1700 deg/s (sharpness 25 -> 40), slide-out sharpness 10 -> 16. Client: a peek shot / throw waits for the full peek pose with the body within `PeekFireMaxAngle` 10 deg of the aim (any aimed / hip shot out of cover waits for the facing); a click is held `FireBufferTime` 0.6 s, a held trigger fires the moment it's ready; blind fire and shots mid vault / dash stay instant. Server: CombatManager refuses a shot while the shooter's `CoverState` is "Turn", or further than `FireFacingTolerance` 22 deg (+ parallax `atan(2 / shot length)`; blind / mid-move 115) from every facing in the shooter's lag window, after one 0.1 s recheck; logged in Studio. GadgetManager refuses a "Turn" throw. Bots turn before they fire. The shot's facing (`FaceYaw`) reaches every client before the damage, and their AimPoseController turns the shooter's body to it at once. |
