# House 1965 test-site restyle: DONE (modeler, 2026-10-28)

Edward: "the house is too clean for a shootout". The House art is restyled from a clean modern suburb into a gritty 1965
nuclear test-site house, styled on the vault card **Map Styles / Test Site House** and `notes/house_1965_style.md` (map
trainer). Own models, textures, patterns and words; no reference asset, logo, poster or lettering. Everything is in
`art/house/v2/` with the **same file names and layout as v1**, so the map builder's swap is drop-in.

Sheets: `notes/house_1965_sheet.jpg` (reference vs ours per room + window views), `notes/house_1965_props_sheet.jpg`
(every piece next to the 5-stud human). Before / after: `captures/house/1965/before_after_{foyer,street}.png`.

## What is final and did not move
The layout, room shell, door and window openings, cover positions and collision are unchanged: v2 builds the shell from
the same `notes/maps/house_layout.json` v1, and every collision box comes from v1.
- **Footprint proof:** `python art/house/v2/tools/footprint_diff.py` compares v1 `art/house/house_furniture.json` with v2
  for every v1 assembly and every room variant against its base:
  `cover pieces (incl. variants): 42, differences: 0 (tol 0.005)`, `PASS: every collision box and every cover footprint unchanged`.
  The size, the bbox about the pivot and every collision box (size + centre) match within 0.005, including the height bands
  (back 3.4 / arms 2.5-2.6 / seat 1.65-1.95 / mattress 2.4 / table 2.6) and the 3.4 / 8.0 cover heights.
- Dressing that has PCOL walk blockers keeps its v1 size within 0.03. That covers the coffee table, nightstands, desks,
  desk chair, vanity, curtain, toilet, washer, stools, lamps, plants and toys.
- Pivots: `texel_pivot_check.py`: **0 mismatches of 116** assemblies.

## What changed
**Furniture.** Every H_* is rebuilt in 1960s shapes inside its v1 box. Every piece is worn: dust film on tops, grime in
creases, chipped enamel and paint edges, stains, torn vinyl and tweed, burns. Cover pieces get closed bases (skirts,
plinths, kick boards).
- **Living room:** the sofa is a skirted cream-vinyl sectional with a tufted back and olive bolsters. The armchairs are olive tweed club chairs.
- **Media and bedroom pieces:** a console TV and radiogram, walnut lowboys, a walnut panelled bed wall with a mustard headboard, a walnut bed with a chenille spread and a maple spindle single bed with a cowboy print.
- **Divider and storage:** a spindle room divider, glass-front bookcases, walnut wardrobes and a cream linen press.
- **Kitchen:** harvest-gold enamel with chrome bar pulls and laminate tops with a chrome edge. A cream range with coil burners, a rounded fridge with a walnut cabinet over it, twin steel wall ovens, a mustard / cream diner booth, chrome stools.
- **Dining room:** a chrome-edged walnut table set for six (plates, glasses, a roast under a dome) with vinyl-and-chrome dinette chairs.
- **Bathroom:** a built-in tub with a tiled apron, mint fittings, a medicine cabinet and a vinyl shower curtain.
- **Era swaps:** a percolator, a whistling kettle, a toaster and bread bin (no microwave), a portable TV and a typewriter.
- **Art:** gold-framed oils, a mid-century print, a kid's rocket drawing, a shag rug and braided rugs.

22 optional room variants (same boxes): FY telephone table and sputnik chandelier, LR credenza, DR china hutch and buffet,
MR painted lockers and drill notice, BH hall tree, MB slipper chair / mesa oil / oval rug, BR2 maple bed, dresser,
wardrobe, bookcase and pennant, BR3 cream chest and wardrobe and school desk, UB butter tub, toilet and vanity.

**Shell.** Each room has its own wall bands, a lower band to a split height with an upper band above:

| Foyer, cross hall, back hall, dining room, landing, upper hall | walnut wainscot | sprig or vine paper |

- **Textures:** they are mapped from each storey's floor, so grime bands and the frieze sit at the right height.
- **Trims:** baseboard, chair rail, picture and plate rails and crown are cut from the actual wall pieces, so they stop at every opening and tuck behind the door casings.
- **Floors:** herringbone parquet, two checker linos, gold and greige carpet, carpet tiles and hex tile. The ceiling is yellowed.
- **Windows:** white-painted 2-over-2 sashes and white sills.
- **Tape crosses:** on 11 windows, one per sash. They are parented to the window's `_Glass`, so the breakable-glass script hides them with the pane.
- **Doors:** flush birch room doors with brass knobs; a walnut front door with three stepped lites; a 6-lite white back door. All keep their names and hinge origins. Door frames are dark walnut casings (white outside).
- **Stairs:** walnut with a moss runner and brass rods (main); painted treads with rubber nosings (back). Railings have turned walnut balusters and newels.
- **Outside:** white lap siding on a fieldstone base, shingles on the existing roof shape, white trim.

**Wear and dressing (all art, no collision):**
- **Wear layer:** 78 decals 0.13 off their surface: water stains (walls and ceilings), peeling paper, scorch marks, bullet-hole clusters, faded rectangles, chalk test marks (our wording), floor scuffs, dust, cracked lino, and sand fans at the outer doors.
- **Drapes:** pulled to the sides of the windows, never across an opening, on brass rods.
- **Cobwebs and sand:** cobwebs in the ceiling corners; sand drifts at the doors and under four windows.
- **Posters and notices:** two posters, two calendars ("MARCH 1965", crossed off to the 15th), a drill notice, a clipboard and a pennant, some tilted.

- **Test dummies:** six faceless chalk-grey dummies with stencilled numbers and period clothes, taller and thinner than an R15. Five are seated: on the sofa, two at the dining table, in the booth, on BR2's bed. One leans on the north kitchen counter, behind spawn B's look direction; it is in no doorway or arch.

**Exterior.**

## For the map builder (installer notes, the swap)
1. Import `H_Furniture_kit`, `H_House` and `H_Exterior` from `art/house/v2/fbx` the same way as v1:
   - Import 3D, Creator = Gunsy Funsy, scale 0.01, Insert Using Scene Position;
   - measure `K_ScaleCube` = 10.
2. **Do not import / use v1's `H_Trees`** with this exterior: the green trees don't fit a desert. The v2 dry trees are `DryTree_XX`, inside `H_Exterior`.
3. **Shower curtain:** `tools/house_installer.lua` line ~494 makes `ShowerGlass` parts 70 % transparent. The v2 curtain is opaque vinyl: drop that line (the PCOL blocker stays).
4. **Wear decals:** `Wear_G` / `Wear_U` use a base-colour texture with alpha. Set `CastShadow = false`. If Import 3D gives them a SurfaceAppearance, set `AlphaMode = Transparency`.
5. **Tape crosses:** `Win_*_GlassTape` arrive as children of `Win_*_Glass`. `takeFromShell` moves them with the pane, so they hide when it breaks. Check this once after the import.
6. **Optional room looks:** apply `house_1965_variants.json` (layout id -> model). If you use `H_Bathtub_Butter`, add it to `ART_ABOVE`: its tap rises above the box, like the base tub.
7. **Walk blockers:** `house_dressing65.json` lists three pieces a player could walk into: the leaning dummy at the kitchen counter, the knocked-over lamp, the knocked-over chair. Add PCOL blockers if wanted.
8. **Lighting:** practical-light meshes keep v1's names (`*_Shade`, `*_Bulb`). The chandelier variant's bulbs are `H_PendantSputnik_Bulbs`. Lighting values: see the style brief (hazy desert afternoon).
9. **Sky:** `tex/sky/sky_side.png` (Bk / Ft / Lf / Rt), `sky_up.png`, `sky_dn.png`: images under the group, as in v1.
